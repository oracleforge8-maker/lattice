## Section 1: Job Search Strategy Prompts

### Prompt 1: Job Search Strategy
```
Act as a senior career coach. I'm a [YOUR PROFESSION] with [X] years of experience looking to transition into [TARGET ROLE/INDUSTRY]. Help me create a comprehensive 30-day job search plan that includes:
1. Daily action items
2. Networking strategy
3. Application targets
4. Skill development priorities
5. Timeline with milestones

My current skills: [LIST YOUR SKILLS]
My target salary: [SALARY RANGE]
My location: [LOCATION or REMOTE]
```

## Section 4: Interview Preparation Prompts

### Prompt 9: Interview Question Generator
```
Generate 20 likely interview questions for a [JOB TITLE] role at a [COMPANY TYPE] company. Include:
1. 5 behavioral questions (STAR format)
2. 5 technical/skill-based questions
3. 5 situational questions
4. 5 culture fit questions

For each question, provide:
- Why the interviewer asks this
- Key points to include in my answer
- A sample strong answer
```

### Prompt 10: STAR Method Coach
```
Help me craft a STAR-format answer for this interview question:

Question: [INTERVIEW QUESTION]

Here's my rough story/experience: [DESCRIBE YOUR EXPERIENCE]

Structure my answer with:
- Situation (2-3 sentences)
- Task (1-2 sentences)
- Action (3-4 sentences, most detail)
- Result (2-3 sentences, quantified)
```

### Prompt 11: Mock Interviewer
```
Act as an interviewer for a [JOB TITLE] position. Conduct a mock interview with me:
1. Ask one question at a time
2. Wait for my response
3. Provide constructive feedback
4. Suggest improvements
5. Ask a follow-up question

Start with a behavioral question.
```
