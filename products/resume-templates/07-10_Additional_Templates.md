# Templates 7-10: Quick-Start Versions

## Template 7: One-Page Concise
*Condensed version of Template 1 — single page, bullet-focused*

Best for: Experienced professionals who need to fit everything on one page.

Key differences from Template 1:
- Remove "Core Competencies" section (integrate into summary)
- Limit to 3-4 bullets per role
- Smaller margins, 10-11pt font
- Education at bottom, condensed

---

## Template 8: Functional Skills-Based
*Emphasizes skills over chronological history — ideal for career changers*

Best for: Career changers, employment gaps, or diverse work history.

Structure:
```
[NAME & CONTACT]

PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
[2-3 sentences]

SKILLS & EXPERTISE
• [Skill Category 1]: [Specific skills]
• [Skill Category 2]: [Specific skills]
• [Skill Category 3]: [Specific skills]

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE
[Grouped by skill, not by job]
• [Achievement demonstrating Skill 1]
• [Achievement demonstrating Skill 2]
• [Achievement demonstrating Skill 3]

WORK HISTORY
[Company, Title, Dates — brief list]

EDUCATION
[Degree, School, Year]
```

---

## Template 9: Academic/Research
*CV format for academic, research, or scientific positions*

Best for: PhDs, researchers, professors, scientists.

Additional sections to include:
- Publications
- Research Experience
- Teaching Experience
- Grants & Fellowships
- Conference Presentations
- Professional Memberships

---

## Template 10: International
*Formatted for international applications*

Best for: Applying outside your home country.

Additional sections:
- Visa/Work Authorization Status
- Language Proficiency (with levels)
- International Experience
- Country-specific formatting notes

---

## 🎯 How to Maximize These Templates

1. **Use the AI prompts** in `AI_Career_Prompts.md` to generate optimized content
2. **Tailor each application** — don't send the same resume everywhere
3. **Quantify everything** — numbers stand out to recruiters
4. **Match keywords** from the job description
5. **Keep it to 1-2 pages** unless applying for executive/academic roles
6. **Proofread** — then proofread again
7. **Get feedback** from someone in your target industry
